Our Story

Real seafood, a breeze off the water and the easy rhythm of a beach day, right here in Lubumbashi.

You’ll find Catch inside Complexe La Plage on Route du Golf. Take a table on the covered terrace facing the sand and Lac Kipopo, or settle in by the long bar indoors, where a big circular aquarium keeps you company. Only fish swim in there; the steaks stay on the grill.

We’re open for lunch and dinner, seven days a week. Come evening the lights go low and the terrace turns quietly romantic.
